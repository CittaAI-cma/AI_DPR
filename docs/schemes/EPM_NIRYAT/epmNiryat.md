## 2. Reference

Confirm live EPM / Niryat Protsahan guidelines for eligible HSNs and subvention tenure.

## 3. Form questions (7 steps)

1. Cover (`exportMarkets`, `hsnLines`, `prePostShipment`, `exportCreditSought`, `entrepreneurName`)  
2. Unit / export profile  
3. Applicant  
4. Export credit / WC need  
5. Means of finance (subvention ≠ capital grant)  
6. Operating & export sales  
7. Uploads: IEC, shipping / LC evidence, Udyam, bank statements
