8. What we may / must not say

After Stages 1–3 we may say:

  • We do not keep identity scans or cluster photos as public web links.
  • Unused reports are cleaned 12 months after last edit (with a 15-day warning).
  • Each audit row is dropped after 24 months.
  • AI help is optional and does not receive KYC files.

We must not say: “We comply with DPDP,” “fully DPDP aligned,” or treat this checklist as a lawyer’s certificate. Stage 4 remains open.
